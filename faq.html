FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Straight answers about pricing, service, scheduling, photos, privacy and SMS policy.

How does pricing work?

Choose an estimated truck load: 1/4 truck $350, 1/2 truck $550, 3/4 truck $625 or full truck $750. Final price and any specialty fees are confirmed before work begins.

What items do you not accept?

Hazardous, regulated, unsafe or prohibited materials may be declined. Send a photo or description and we will confirm before scheduling.

Do you offer light demolition?

Yes. We quote approved non-structural tear-outs and cleanup separately after reviewing photos, access and disposal needs.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. Mama Got Junk is insured and bonded.

What happens to reusable items?

We prioritize donation, reuse and responsible disposal whenever practical.

Can I upload photos?

Yes. Both the smart quote form and chat accept up to four photos and compress them before sending.

What is the cancellation policy?

Any cancellation fee or special scheduling term will be disclosed before it applies. We may reschedule when access, safety, weather or job details prevent service.

How do text messages work?

SMS consent is optional and not required to buy. Message frequency varies; rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Read the full Terms & SMS Policy and Privacy Policy linked below.

Why is the business address not on every page?

To protect a home-based business location, the address is not shown on marketing pages. It remains available on legal policy pages where needed for business and A2P compliance.
